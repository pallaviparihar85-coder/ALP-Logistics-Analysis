# 📦 ALP Logistics Analysis Dashboard

##  Project Overview

The ALP Logistics Analysis Dashboard is an interactive Business Intelligence project developed using Python and Streamlit. The dashboard analyzes logistics and supply chain data to evaluate sales performance, profitability, customer value, product performance, and the impact of discounts on profit margins. It provides interactive visualizations, KPIs, business insights, and recommendations to support data-driven decision-making.

---

##  Problem Statement

Despite having detailed order and sales data, the organization lacks:

- Visibility into profitability by customer and product.
- Understanding of discount-driven margin erosion.
- Identification of high-value and low-value customers.
- Market and category-level profit diagnostics.
- Interactive reporting for better business decision-making.

---

##  Project Objectives

- Analyze sales and profit performance across different markets.
- Identify high-value and low-value customers.
- Evaluate product and category profitability.
- Measure the impact of discounts on profit margins.
- Build an interactive Business Intelligence dashboard using Streamlit.
- Generate business insights and actionable recommendations.

##  Key Business Insights

- Sales and profitability vary across different markets.
- A small group of customers contributes the majority of business profit.
- Some customers generate low profitability and require strategic attention.
- Higher discount rates generally reduce profit margins.
- Product profitability differs across categories.
- Market-wise analysis helps identify profitable business regions.

---

##  Business Recommendations

- Retain high-value customers through loyalty programs.
- Review pricing strategies for low-profit products.
- Minimize excessive discounting on low-margin products.
- Focus on high-performing product categories.
- Monitor market-wise profitability regularly for better business decisions.
